## Research findings

- Gemma 4 (released 2026) replaced Gemma 3n as the local audio model. E2B,
  E4B and 12B Unified accept audio (ASR and speech translation), 30 s maximum
  per request, 140+ languages. Google publishes ASR and AST prompt templates;
  the AST template returns transcript and translation in one answer.
- llama.cpp `llama-server` serves Gemma 4 audio from GGUF plus mmproj through
  the OpenAI-compatible `/v1/chat/completions` with `input_audio` blocks
  (support added in 2026; the server dispatch gap was fixed). Pre-quantized
  models at `ggml-org/gemma-4-E2B-it-GGUF` (Q4_0 2.84 GB, Q8_0 4.97 GB, mmproj
  BF16 0.99 GB) and `gemma-4-E4B-it-GGUF` (Q4_0 4.59 GB, Q8_0 8.03 GB).
- vLLM serves Gemma 4 audio with `audio_url` blocks; recipe recommends 24 GB+
  NVIDIA GPUs.
- Ollama does not support audio input for Gemma models.
- Conclusion: the whole codebase can avoid Python; inference is an HTTP
  sidecar. Gemini fits the same interface.
- create-t3-app in 2026: Next.js 15 App Router, tRPC v11, Tailwind v4, Drizzle
  or Prisma, NextAuth or Better Auth, ESLint or Biome.
- Development laptop: Intel i7-1255U, 15 GB RAM, no discrete GPU. Node 22,
  pnpm 10, Go 1.24, Docker 29, ffmpeg present.

## Decisions taken by the agent while writing (for review)

- `translationMode: ast` as default rather than ASR plus text translation,
  because Google's AST prompt yields both texts in one audio call. Recorded as
  ADR-003; the plan had listed the reverse default.
- Postgres + Drizzle, Biome, env-password admin auth, SSE via tRPC, direct
  browser-to-pipeline WebSocket ingest, transparent web page for OBS. All in
  [decisions.md](../decisions.md).
- Palette: violet primary, cyan secondary, green/orange/yellow/magenta/grey
  accents, coral for errors, on near-black. Caption font Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Fixture audio to be recorded or synthesized by the team rather than taken
  from public corpora, to keep licensing trivial.
