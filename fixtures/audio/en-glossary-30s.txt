Before we go live, a quick look at the toolbox. We deploy every change with kubectl, and the cluster state lives in etcd. This week at Nerdearla, several speakers demoed kubectl plugins on stage. If kubectl hangs, check etcd health first. Nerdearla runs for four days, and every room streams captions like these. When etcd is slow, kubectl feels slow too, so we watch both during the talks at Nerdearla.
